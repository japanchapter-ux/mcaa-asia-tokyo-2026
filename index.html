About the Regional Meeting

The MCAA Asia Connect “Konnichiwa Tokyo 2026” gathers leaders and researchers from the Marie Curie Alumni Association (MCAA) chapters in Japan, India, China, and Southeast Asia, along with delegates from Oceania. Celebrating 30 years of the MCAA, the meeting strengthens Asia-Pacific regional integration, promotes multilateral science diplomacy, bridges Japanese deep-tech startups with academic research, and formalizes our regional governance charter.
